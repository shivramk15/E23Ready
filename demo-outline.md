# E23Ready — 5-minute demo outline

Audience: a model-risk manager or TPRM analyst at a Canadian bank. Goal: get
them to run a pack themselves and send corrections back.

## 0:00–0:45 — The problem (no slides, one sentence each)
- Every AI tool at the bank needs an E-23 inventory entry by May 1, 2027.
- Your team is doing intake in spreadsheets, one vendor at a time.
- Each new Copilot/Cursor/dbt-AI rollout takes quarters because nobody has the vendor's paperwork pre-triaged.

## 0:45–2:30 — The live run
- `python3 e23ready.py assess --answers sample-answers.yaml --out output`
- Show the terminal summary: tier HIGH, control counts, board verdict.
- Key line: "Nothing left the building. No procurement, no signup, no data shared."

## 2:30–4:00 — The four artifacts
- Open `04-board-ready-one-pager.md`: "this is the cover page your boss reads."
- Open `03-b10-due-diligence-gap-report.md`: "the red rows are your negotiation list."
- Open `01-model-inventory-entry.md`: "this is the E-23 entry your examiner wants to see."
- Open `02-e23-risk-tier-classification.md`: "worst-of-four-dimensions — same logic your framework uses."

## 4:00–5:00 — The ask (gift, not pitch)
- "Here's the Copilot pack. Tell me what's wrong with it — your corrections become version 0.2."
- "Which vendor is your team onboarding next? I'll triage that pack first."

## Follow-up (not part of the 5 minutes)
- Send the repo link + pack file; collect their corrected statuses.
- Success metric from the 30-day plan: 10 packs generated by real bank staff,
  3 model-risk teams asking for a walkthrough.
