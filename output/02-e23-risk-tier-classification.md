## Method

Per OSFI Guideline E-23 (effective May 1, 2027), third-party AI/ML systems are
classified across four dimensions. The overall tier is the **worst** of the
four dimension ratings.

## Dimension ratings

| Dimension | Rating | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| complexity | MODERATE |
| autonomy | HIGH |
| data_sensitivity | MODERATE |
| customer_impact | HIGH |

**Overall tier: HIGH** — driven by worst dimension (complexity=moderate, autonomy=high, data_sensitivity=moderate, customer_impact=high)

## What the tier triggers

- **LOW:** standard vendor onboarding; include in annual inventory review.
- **MODERATE:** documented risk acceptance by the owning line of business; re-assess on material model change.
- **HIGH:** model-risk committee review; enhanced monitoring plan; contractual controls (audit rights, incident SLA, exit) required before enterprise rollout.
