## How to read the combined column

- **OK** — control met on both sides; re-confirm quarterly.
- **PARTIAL** — met in part; confirm with vendor and get contract language.
- **GAP** — missing on at least one side; remediate or document formal risk acceptance before rollout.
- **UNVERIFIED** — no usable evidence; obtain it from the trust center / DPA first.
